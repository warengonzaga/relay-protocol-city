    Loading a little world of movement…

    A city that moves with Relay.

    Chains are districts. Relay app trips move house to house; reported integrators move between their buildings. One traveler is one request, and its ride is chosen by its USD value.

    This preview uses illustrative transactions. Connect a verified Relay feed to watch real activity.

    Toll gates represent the handoff from origin to fill. Pending trips wait; failed trips return; BLOCKED and BLOCKED_WALLET outcomes get a playful police escort. These are stage illustrations, not exact fund locations. Completed requests replay their journey.

    Pedestrians use sidewalks. Same-chain trips run a local loop. Other walkers run when measured or estimated duration is 15 seconds or less. Animation speed is scaled for viewing, not a settlement timer.

    Trains enter through the yard, stop at both chain stations, and return to the tunnel. Each direction carries one train; the rest queue.
